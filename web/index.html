How multi-broker trading will work

Market research and AI trade explanations can be shared. Cash, positions, order history, pending-order checks, rate limits and maximum-loss rules will be isolated by broker and account. Choosing another broker will never redirect a Schwab order to that broker automatically.

Webull and Alpaca require their respective developer authorization and approval. Robinhood's publicly documented API is for crypto trading, not general stock trading. Until a provider's connection is implemented, this page shows its official developer requirements rather than accepting passwords or pretending it is connected.

Configure or test the forecast service · Forecast installation guide

How are forecast candidates chosen, and what do the numbers mean?

Forecasting works natively on TrueNAS without a second AI computer. It scans a 24-symbol predefined universe using up to two years of daily historical prices and volume. Candidates need at least 180 historical bars, $5–$25 latest close, 200,000+ average daily shares over 20 sessions and recent market data.

Built-in mode: daily returns, 5/20/50-session moving averages, 20-session volatility, relative volume, prior highs/lows, deterministic risk reference levels and a descriptive daily-volatility band. Selected AI provider: optional Ollama/ChatGPT/Gemini qualitative review of public headlines. Advanced mode: optional separate LightGBM, Chronos-Bolt and FinBERT. Source-linked news and unmet confirmations appear per candidate when available.

NAS-native analysis updates its dated research snapshot periodically. An optional advanced workstation uses its own model refresh schedule. Clicking Refresh display loads the latest available snapshot; it does not submit orders or retrain a specialized model. Quote spreads and live bid/ask checks are separately sourced from Schwab only when the read-only monitor is connected.

Research only: ranked ideas and price scenarios are not calibrated profit probabilities or permission to submit live orders.

How this works

Changes are written atomically to the server. The bot reads them on its next cycle. Already-open paper positions are not resized, and prior trades are not rewritten.

Changing risk does not imply any predicted return. AI and external market data are separate integrations.
