What does the forecast analyze?

The NAS-native analysis screens a fixed 24-stock research universe using up to two years of daily prices and volume, latest close $5–$25, 20-session average volume 200,000+ shares, sufficient bars and recent data. It scores momentum, trend, relative volume and volatility using reproducible formulas. Its historical-volatility band is an illustrative range, not a calibrated prediction.

Inputs include daily returns, 5/20/50-session trend comparisons, 10/30-session volatility, relative volume, recent highs and lows, and timestamped financial-news headlines.

Selected AI provider: when enabled, reviews source-dated public headlines for sentiment, catalysts and downside risks only. Optional advanced mode: LightGBM checks a return estimate against chronological holdout, Chronos-Bolt displays scenario quantiles and FinBERT classifies headline sentiment. Neither cloud nor local AI is permitted to fabricate historical measurements or trigger orders.

Built-in analyses refresh periodically on the NAS and preserve their last completed dated snapshot. The advanced AI computer runs on its separate scheduled refresh. Neither mode produces executable prices, calibrated profitability predictions or broker-order authorization.

How to install or troubleshoot the forecast service

Most users can skip this. Built-in forecasting requires only the Auto Trader app and outbound access to supported historical market-data feeds. For advanced models, install Python 3.12 on a separate machine, download the repository's ai-workstation requirements and run forecast_service.py. Model weights stay on that machine.

Set the AI computer's AUTOTRADER_AI_BIND to its private LAN IP and AUTOTRADER_AI_PORT to 8766. Restrict the Windows firewall port to your TrueNAS address. Verify http://AI_COMPUTER_IP:8766/health and then click Test forecast connection. The forecast endpoint is /api/forecast; the dashboard URL field should contain only the host and port.
