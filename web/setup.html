WELCOME TO AUTO TRADER

Set up your trading workstation

One app. Configure your administrator account, AI connection and optional Schwab access here. No trading begins during installation.

1. Claim this installation

In TrueNAS → Apps → Auto Trader → Workloads → Logs, copy the one-time setup code. You won't need the shell.

2. Select your AI

Use the OAuth sign-in on your own AI computer. Add its companion URL and connection token here; no cloud API key or account password is required. OAuth sign-in must be authorized separately.

3. Schwab account (optional)

Use your Schwab Developer app key and secret. After installation, click “Connect Schwab account” and sign in at Schwab's website. Never enter your brokerage password here.

4. Choose modules

Live broker orders remain disabled. You can change connections later from Profile → Integrations.
