WELCOME TO AUTO TRADER

Create your account and choose AI

Just two steps: protect your dashboard and select an AI model. Broker accounts and risk settings can be configured later in their own workspace. No live trading starts during installation.

1. Claim this installation

In TrueNAS → Apps → Auto Trader → Workloads → Logs, copy the one-time setup code. You won't need the shell.

2. Select your AI

Use the OAuth sign-in on your own AI computer. Add its companion URL and connection token here; no cloud API key or account password is required. OAuth sign-in must be authorized separately.

Forecasting works automatically with the built-in NAS research engine; specialized models are optional and can be selected later under AI & Forecast Settings.

To connect Charles Schwab or other trading accounts, open Broker Connections after creating this administrator account.

Real-money orders are not enabled by installation.
